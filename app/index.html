Before you start — two things to know

1 · One trophy can be missed

Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot across Leonida, 18 spots in all — is the only trophy on the list that can be missed. Lucia’s balcony meditation spot is only there in Chapter 1; once that chapter ends it is gone, and there is no mission replay and no New Game Plus. Meditate there before Chapter 1 ends, and hold on to a manual Chapter 1 save until the trophy pops.

2 · Save by hand, chapter by chapter

There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus. Make a manual save at the start of every chapter and one more before the final mission — the game gives you 15 manual save slots plus 1 autosave. Cheats turn off trophies and saving, so leave them alone on a trophy run.

Partner trophies need Jason and Lucia together: switching between them opens after the Breakfast Run mission in Chapter 2. Take your partner to every outdoor meditation spot as well — each spot should count for both characters at once.

The road to the Platinum — around 200 hours

Play in this order. The story delivers about half the list; the rest is cleanup once the final mission is done. Work finished before the story ends still counts toward 100%, so start the long grinds early wherever a guide says to. Each stage below shows how many of its trophies you have earned so far.

  1. Stage 1 — Chapters 1 & 2: the story opens

    5 trophies. Play the story. In Chapter 1, meditate at Lucia’s balcony spot before the chapter ends — it is the missable one. Save by hand at the start of every chapter. After Breakfast Run in Chapter 2 you can switch between Jason and Lucia, which is what the partner trophies need.

    Just Like Paradise · Paradise Lost · T**s Ass Drug Money · Trust · Positive Mental Attitude (missable)

  2. Stage 2 — Chapters 3 & 4: the story widens

    7 trophies. Jewelry jobs open in Chapter 3 (Sneakthief belongs to Stage 3). Near the end of Chapter 4 the optional bank-vault mission turns up — take it; it feeds Maximum Withdrawal, Hot Together, A Classic Blitz Play and Dime on the Dollar. Save by hand before the final mission.

    Vice City, Baby! · Top Of The World, Ma · Break the Bank · Family Matters · DWNFALL · Hi-Jacked · Snow Storm

  3. Stage 3 — Strands, robberies & the finale

    17 trophies. Finish the stranger strands (8 count toward Strangers & Freaks — Andy’s, Billy’s and Tyrone’s do not, they have trophies of their own), the character finales, the robberies and the partner trophies. Buy the 8 garages and register 10 vehicles. Then play the final mission.

    You Gotta Know When to Get Off · Road to Rehabilitation · This Was No Boat Accident · The Lone Rider · Supply & Demand · Spin On This! · Fair Game · Strangers & Freaks · Sneakthief · Maximum Withdrawal · A Classic Blitz Play · Hot Together · Retail Therapy · Dime on the Dollar · Pest Control · Little Boxes · On the Spot

  4. Stage 4 — Activities

    17 trophies. Shooting Range golds (42), drone races (14 locations), 20 different race wins, fishing against Cal, one arcade game, Combat Focus takedowns, and the one-off stunt trophies (plane, tank, showroom, bowling, cocktail).

    Master of None · The Shootist · Top Fun · Earn Cash, Not Kudos · Baited My Tackle · Stick Fiddler · Gunslinger · Catch Me If You Can · Challenging Tank · In The Beginning… · Capital Offense · Let’s Go Bowling · Personal Best · Bad Luck, I Guess · The Truth Is Out There · Ten Pin · Treat Yo Self

  5. Stage 5 — Post-story cleanup & 100%

    9 trophies. The 100% checklist turns up under Progress > Stats after the story. The long poles: 300 stunts, 70 of 80 challenges, 200 of 277 vehicles, all 39 illegal operations, 3 card sets, 100 WAiNK locations, 52 areas, 500 profile actions. A Long Road (gold) pops just before the platinum.

    Vice Got a Price! · A Long Road · Cunning Stunts · Don’t Touch My Deck · You WAiNKer! · One Last Thing · Whip It Good · The Goodtime State · Actions Speak Louder
